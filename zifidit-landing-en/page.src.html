Shipping policy

Our fulfillment partner

To keep quality high and international delivery fast, Zifidit works with EPROLO, a global fulfillment and shipping company that runs more than 26 certified warehouses across the US, UK, Europe and Asia.

Quality check and tracking

Every order is quality-checked by our fulfillment partner before it ships. You'll receive a tracking number by email within 3–5 business days of payment confirmation.

Delivery guarantee

  • United States, Europe, Canada and New Zealand: if your parcel hasn't arrived within 35 days of shipping, we'll refund you in full or send a free replacement.
  • Middle East, Africa, South America and India: we guarantee delivery within 65 days of shipping.

Delivery confirmation, address and customs

Once tracking shows “Delivered”, Zifidit is no longer responsible for the parcel. Please enter your shipping address and phone number carefully. We can't compensate for orders lost because of an incorrect address or a parcel that wasn't collected from the local carrier. Any customs duties charged by your country are paid by the customer.

Refund & return policy

Damaged, defective or wrong items

If your item arrives damaged, incomplete or different from what you ordered, email info@zifidit.com within 10 days of delivery. Include your order number and clear photos or a short video showing the problem. We'll send a free replacement or refund you in full right away, and you won't need to ship the item back.

Not covered

  • Parcels that tracking shows as “Delivered”.
  • Parcels not collected from the delivery company.
  • Parcels refused at customs.
  • A wrong shipping address entered at checkout.
  • Change of mind, or not liking an item's look or smell when it arrived intact and as described.

Contact

Email us at info@zifidit.com with your order number and we'll get back to you.
